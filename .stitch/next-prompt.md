A high-security, enterprise unified login and role-gateway portal for "Factory ERP: Custom Hairpiece Manufacturing & Order Management System".

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web Application, Desktop-optimized with responsive tablet support
- Theme: Dark Industrial Precision with Bespoke Atelier Gold
- Background: Deep Midnight Slate `#090E17`, Card Surfaces `#131B2B`, Borders `#28354D`
- Primary Accent: Polished Amber Gold `#F59E0B`, Burnished Gold `#D97706`
- Status Colors: Emerald Verified `#10B981`, Amber Warning `#F97316`, Crimson Rush `#EF4444`
- Typography: Headers in Outfit/Inter, Body in Inter, Technical Codes/Measurements/Prices in JetBrains Mono
- Shapes: Modern crisp corners (4px to 10px radius), hairline mechanical borders, zero gratuitous heavy drop-shadows
- Tone: High-density B2B Enterprise ERP & MES, Authoritative, Functional, Artisanal Quality

**Page Structure & Core Requirements:**
1. **Header & System Brand:**
   - Logo mark: "Factory ERP" with mechanical precision badge & subtitle: "Bespoke Hairpiece Manufacturing & Order System".
   - Status telemetry indicator: "SERVER STATUS: OPERATIONAL (MELBOURNE/SEOUL HUB)".
2. **Unified Login Panel (Centered Card):**
   - Business ID / Email & Password input fields with dark input styling and focused amber gold ring.
   - 2FA / Security Code field with monospaced digit formatting.
   - "Remember Business Terminal" checkbox & "Forgot Credentials / Request B2B Account" links.
   - Primary "Authenticate & Access Portal" action button in solid polished amber gold (`#F59E0B`).
3. **Role & Workspace Selector (Interactive Demo / Quick Switcher):**
   - 4 distinct role cards illustrating the partitioned entry points:
     * **Client Portal:** For B2B salons & clinics (Order placement, real-time tracking, invoices).
     * **Admin Console:** Operations & executive hub (Full order acceptance, pricing tiers, ledger oversight).
     * **MES Line Operator:** Factory floor & barcode scanning (Job orders, QR scanner, QC inspection).
     * **Accounting:** Finance & settlement (A/R ledger, payment reconciliation, tax invoices).
4. **Footer & Trust Indicators:**
   - Multi-tenant data isolation badge: "AES-256 Encrypted • Multi-Tenant RBAC Partitioning".
   - System version `v2.4.0-MES`, copyright notice, and emergency operations hotline.
